Body marks · round two · eight candidates Round one, geometry only

Round one had no body in it. These eight put one back, in two treatments: a drawn skeleton and a solid figure. Four are body alone. Four fuse the figure into a round-one mark, so the geometry still carries the mechanism while the body says, immediately, that this is about lifting.

Why a figure is the right move here

This is not a decorative choice. The product spec already names the skeleton overlay as the brand's visual signature (§3.2: use the real joint overlay as a first-class design element, derived from actual pose data, not a generic icon, because it is the one thing a copycat cannot fake). The app draws exactly this figure on the report screen. A mark built from it is the identity agreeing with the product rather than decorating it.

Everything below is a squat at depth, seen from the front. That is deliberate. It is the exercise the engine measures best, it is the only view in which knee travel is visible, and it is the pose the whole app is built around.

Where I would go

Recommendation

06 Framed is the one I would build on. It is the only mark in either round that draws the product's hardest constraint, the rule that the whole body including the feet must stay inside the calibrated volume, and it turns a limitation into the identity. It also fixes round one's weakness: it has a body and it still carries the camera.

12 The Chain is the reduction play. Three dots and two lines is very little design to defend, but it is unmistakably a limb, it is the app's own rep-strip glyph promoted, and it is the only mark in either round that still reads at 12px.

10 Bottom Position and 11 The Lifter are the same idea in the two treatments you asked to compare. Pick 10 if the brand should feel like an instrument company, 11 if it should feel like a gym. Neither says anything about measurement, which is the trade.

09 Sightlines is the weakest and I would not ship it. It was recomposed three times. Overhead cameras made the sightlines read as raised arms twice over, and moving them to the sides fixed that but left the mark cluttered. Two cameras plus a full figure is more than a logo can hold. If you want the mechanism stated, round one's 02 does it cleanly without a body.
