Joule Coach

Logo directions · five candidates

Joule Coach measures a body from two angles at once and tells you the truth about what it saw. None of these marks is a dumbbell, a bicep, a heartbeat or a lightning bolt. Each one is drawn from the engine's real geometry, so the logo says something a competitor cannot copy without owning the same machine.

What the mark has to earn

The product's one unfakeable claim is multi-camera triangulation. Two calibrated views resolve a joint in true 3D, including frontal-plane knee travel that no single camera can see at all. The engine even carries a hard install rule about it: two cameras must converge somewhere between 47° and 133°, and must never sit opposite each other, because measured error roughly doubles at 180°. That constraint is a gift to a logo. It is a real number, it is specific to this product, and it is a shape.

The second thing worth encoding is the tone. Calibrated confidence, not hype. The product's whole credibility rests on drawing what it did not measure as carefully as what it did. Several of these marks carry that as a solid stroke against a dashed one, which is the same convention the app already uses on every screen.

Rejected before drawing

Every one of these is what this category reaches for by default. The lightning bolt is the worst trap, because "Joule" is a unit of energy and the bolt is the first thing anyone draws. It describes the name, not the product.

Palette

The logo introduces no new brand colours. It is built from the palette the product already uses to tell the truth, which is why the mark sits inside the app rather than on top of it.

Where I would go

Recommendation

02 Triangulation is the strongest mark and the one I would build the identity on. It is the only candidate that draws the actual mechanism, it carries the solid-versus-dashed honesty convention in its own construction, and it survives to 16px. Its risk is that it reads as a diagram rather than a logo, which is a real objection.

01 Overlap is the safer commercial choice. It is the most logo-like of the five, scales furthest down, and still means something specific.

05 Plumb J is the one to pick if you want a letterform people can recognise as a letter before they understand anything about the product.
